ZAMAN

Modular Switch‑like retro console (Coming Soon end of 2026)

Zaman is a hybrid gaming console designed for longevity, repairability, and cost-effective upgrades. The system is made of three parts: Core, Wing, and Nest.

Core

The swappable brain — Efficient Processor, RAM, storage, and wireless. Upgradable across generations

Wing

The handheld body — featuring a high refresh rate, 6” display, large battery, and hall-effect controls

Nest

The desktop/console body — Docking station with enough connectivity and advanced cooling that unlocks full processor performance.

About Kawn Electro

Kawn Electro is a solo engineering lab dedicated to modular, repairable, and long-lasting hardware. Every design decision prioritises longevity and interoperability. We reject planned obsolescence in favour of devices that users can upgrade, repair, and reconfigure over time.

Based in Saudi Arabia, the lab is committed to open-ecosystem design principles. Whether developing consumer electronics or smart home devices, the goal remains the same: hardware that respects its owner's intelligence and investment.

Modularity is not a feature — it's a philosophy.

The Founder

Kadhem is a hardware engineer and a tech enthusiast with a passion for modular, repair‑friendly electronics. His work spans embedded systems, smart home gadgets, and industrial devices. Always driven by the belief that great hardware should grow with its user, not be replaced.

With a professional certificate in PCB design (CPCD by PCEA) and a hands‑on approach to prototyping, Kadhem brings both professional experience and maker creativity to every project at Kawn Electro.

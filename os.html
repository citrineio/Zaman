Open foundations

Linux underneath.
Zaman at your fingertips.

The current development installation uses Armbian with Debian 13 . Armbian provides the operating-system foundation and board integration. Zaman brings the library, system UI and the coordination that turns individual applications into a console experience.

Upstream projects keep their own licenses. Zaman’s components are developed in public; each repository documents its own scope.
